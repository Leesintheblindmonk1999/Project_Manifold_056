# Responsible Use Statement

Project Manifold 0.56 and its SAS successor are **structural coherence auditing tools**, not factual oracles.

- They do not detect intent, deception, or "truth" in an absolute sense.
- They measure structural alignment between a source and a generated response.
- They should not be used as the sole basis for high-stakes decisions (medical, legal, safety-critical) without human review.
- They should not be used for automatic censorship of human-generated content.

κD = 0.56 is an operational audit threshold under empirical validation, not a universal physical constant.
